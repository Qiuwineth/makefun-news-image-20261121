# Facts — The World on Saturday, 21 November 2026

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Saturday 21 November 2026
(in some time zone), and is verified against at least two independent trusted
sources. Figures are used on the poster only where the sources agree.

## 1. LEAD — COP31 climate talks deadlocked in overtime (Antalya, Türkiye)

- UN climate talks in Antalya, scheduled to end Friday, stretched deep into
  overtime on Saturday with no deal; a draft agreement had removed all mention
  of fossil fuels and dropped a proposed $120 billion target for finance to
  help developing countries adapt to climate change.
- EU Climate Commissioner Wopke Hoekstra: "It's either going to be a good deal
  or it's going to be no deal." Nearly 200 countries are negotiating.
- Sources: Reuters, "COP31 climate summit risks failure as talks go deep into
  overtime" (21 Nov 2026); Associated Press via Los Angeles Post (21 Nov);
  phys.org / AFP, "UN climate talks enter crunch time with no deal in sight";
  BBC World headlines (21 Nov).

## 2. Iran strike on Bahrain; tankers hit near Hormuz (Gulf)

- Bahrain said an Iranian strike caused a fire at an industrial facility;
  Iranian Guards also claimed missile and drone strikes on US positions in
  Bahrain on Saturday night.
- Two tankers were attacked in the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, according to
  UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) reports; Brent crude jumped to $92.
- Sources: RTM (Malaysia), "Dua kapal tangki diserang di Selat Hormuz" and
  Bahrain coverage (21 Nov); Standard Topic world archive (21 Nov); One Turn
  News (21 Nov); InvestorTrust, "Dua Tanker Dilaporkan Meledak di Selat
  Hormuz, Minyak Brent Melonjak Jadi US$92" (21 Nov).

## 3. Russia strikes Kryvyi Rih; Ukraine hits ship near Finland (Ukraine)

- A Russian ballistic missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, President Zelensky's
  hometown, killed two people and wounded 58, six of them children, on

- Drones struck a Russia-bound cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; Finnish
  forces say Ukraine was likely responsible.
- Sources: One Turn News (21 Nov); AppiSToS world feed, "Zelensky reports 58
  injured in Kryvyi Rih" (21 Nov); Standard Topic (21 Nov); Jabulani Radio

## 6. Magnitude 6.7 earthquake shakes Peru's Andes (Junín)

- A magnitude 6.7 earthquake struck about 30 km northwest of Junín at 129 km
  depth at 00:09 UTC on 21 November (19:09 local, 20 November) and was felt
  in Lima; Peru's civil defence (INDECI), in its balance published on
  21 November, recorded 1 dead, 16 injured and 152 homes uninhabitable.
- Sources: Instituto Geofísico del Perú (IGP) and USGS figures reported by
  Fish4News and LiveFromEurope (21 Nov); INDECI Complementary Report
  No. 16257 (21 Nov, 18:00) via NomadLawyer and Travel and Tour World;
  RTM, "Gempa bumi 6.7 magnitud gegar tengah Peru" (21 Nov).

## 7. UN states agree to negotiate "killer robot" rules (Geneva)

- UN talks in Geneva on lethal autonomous weapons systems ended on Saturday
  with the 128 member states of the Convention on Certain Conventional
  Weapons agreeing to negotiate new rules for such weapons within two years
  (by 2028).
- Sources: Reuters via Mediacorp BERITA, "Anggota PBB setuju runding
  peraturan kawal selia 'robot pemusnah' jelang 2028" (21 Nov 2026);
  ANTARA via wartawan.id, "Sejumlah Negara Sepakat Susun Instrumen
  Internasional Senjata Otonom" (21 Nov 2026).
